## Taste profile

Digest only (~15 bullets max) — full evidence and history live in [[Taste Profile]].

- Loves slow-burn [[Crime]] and [[Thriller]] with a strong sense of place, especially [[Los Angeles]]
- [[Denis Villeneuve]] is a near-automatic yes; cerebral [[Science Fiction]] over spectacle
- Allergic to loud franchise [[Action]] — [[Michael Bay]] is a hard no
- Rewards [[Obsession]] stories: procedural, patient, character-first
